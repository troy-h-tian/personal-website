Selected Writing

A sample of technical, policy, and humanities writing across several domains. View all

01

The Semantic Space Between: American Chinese Cuisine

A senior project paper tracing how the American Chinese food industry has mirrored, shaped, and been shaped by the Chinese American immigrant experience — from the Gold Rush to Panda Express.

02

Selected Essays: Supervised Program for Alignment Research

Five featured responses from a competitive AI safety research fellowship application — covering liability caps, OpenAI's Preparedness Framework, biomarker qualification as an analogy for AI evals, Loss of Control risk factors, and a martingale-based framework for detecting sycophancy in LLM interactions.

03

Algorithmic Monocultures in LLM Political Framing

URC-Sciences program research proposal: do LLM alignment techniques systematically degrade epistemic diversity? A research design using adversarial prompts, LIME/SHAP interpretability, and latent variable decomposition.

04

Intra-Asian Migration and the Limits of the Asian Community

Examines whether the quotidian experiences of intra-Asian migrants — motivated by economic opportunity and socio-cultural affinity — support or preclude the formation of a proposed "Asian Community" modeled on the European Union.

05

Selected Essays: Cambridge Boston Alignment Initiative

Two application responses for the Cambridge Boston Alignment Initiative — on systemic failures in AI capabilities evaluation and the influence of Ajeya Cotra's forecasting analysis on safety epistemology.

06

Leonie Biney's Formation of Black Feminine Totality via Dream-Like, Otherworldly Futures

A video essay analyzing Biney's 2025 single "Groupie" through Afrofuturist, dominance feminist, and Afropessimist frameworks — arguing her thin lyrical personage and salient Black embodiment construct a world of unbounded romantic aliveness.
